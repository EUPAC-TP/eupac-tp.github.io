EUPAC-TP Seminar Series

EUPAC-TP hosts informal online seminars across mathematical and theoretical physics, connecting researchers across continents. Everyone is welcome to participate or present.

In 2025, we focused on Generalised (Categorical) Symmetries, following these lecture notes. In 2026, the series resumed with invited talks across physics.
